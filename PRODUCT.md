# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The site should use structured listing data to generate maintainable regular HTML pages. No framework has been selected.

## Users

Primary users are people in Bengaluru looking for a home to rent or buy. They need to compare a small set of current property options and contact a human without losing the property context. Owners and sellers are a separate supply-side audience.

## Product Purpose

The product is a Bengaluru property discovery and enquiry service for homes and flats for rent and sale. The parent site explains the service and builds trust. A property subdomain publishes current listings and routes enquiries to the client.

## Positioning

The initial position is a curated directory with clearer, fresher listing information and a real person behind every enquiry. This is a proposed launch wedge, not a measured market claim.

## Operating Context

The client is starting the business. Listings will be manually curated at launch and shared through Google Search, WhatsApp, and social media. The parent brand site is BhaivaTech at https://bhaivatech.com/. The desired property catalogue will live on a subdomain, with hosting managed through the client’s Miles Web Hosting account once confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Main site pages should include Home, About, How We Work or How We Verify, renter and buyer guides, and Contact.
- The property subdomain should include a catalogue, useful locality pages, and individual property pages.
- Rent and Buy are co-primary journeys.
- Leads should be collected through a short form and a WhatsApp path.
- Pages should be crawlable, shareable, responsive, accessible, and maintainable.
- The first release should not pretend to be a full marketplace with accounts, owner uploads, or a large live inventory.
- The exact subdomain, listing source, form destination, WhatsApp number, analytics, legal copy, verification definition, launch neighbourhood, and language support remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the BhaivaTech relationship recognizable and reuse the approved parent logo after the exact asset is confirmed. The property experience should feel warm, familiar, transparent, and human rather than corporate or portal-heavy.

## Evidence on Hand

- The user’s product brief and office-hours decisions establish the Bengaluru scope, rent and buy journeys, subdomain plan, SEO goal, WhatsApp distribution, and human content requirement.
- The parent brand site is live at https://bhaivatech.com/.
- No live property inventory, testimonials, verified metrics, client contact routing, or approved legal copy has been provided yet. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Publish only property information the team can support.
2. Make the next human conversation easy and contextual.
3. Add local usefulness, not keyword volume.
4. Keep listing updates simple enough for a new business to maintain.
5. Show uncertainty clearly instead of hiding it behind polished claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible labels, keyboard access, clear focus states, readable contrast, mobile-friendly controls, alt text, reduced motion support, and a clear privacy explanation for lead data. Confirm whether Kannada or Hindi should be supported at launch.
