Why this service exists

The property desk should be easy to trust.

BhaivaTech is building a smaller, clearer way to discover homes in Bengaluru. The first version is deliberately focused on useful details, current listings, and a real person behind the enquiry.

Start with the need, not the noise.

Property search can become a trail of tabs, forwards, repeated questions, and listings that are hard to date. This service is being shaped around a simpler first step: tell us what you are looking for, see what is current, and ask a person when something needs context.

  • Rent and buy journeys that are easy to tell apart.
  • Property pages that put useful facts near the top.
  • Local information that helps a decision, not just a search engine.
  • A clear route to WhatsApp or a short enquiry.

What we plan to publish.

Only the information that helps a renter or buyer decide whether to take the next step.

Current homes

Property type, locality, price or rent, key facts, images, availability notes, and the date the team last checked the listing.

Local context

Useful notes about daily life, commute questions, nearby needs, and tradeoffs that the team can explain from real experience.

Practical guides

Renter and buyer checklists that help visitors prepare for a viewing and ask better questions.

Clear handoff

A short enquiry that carries the visitor’s need and property context to the person who can respond.

What we will not claim.

We will not call a listing verified unless the client defines the check and can support it. We will not publish fake availability, invented market numbers, or a promise that a property is right for everyone.
