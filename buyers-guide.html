Home/Buyers guide

A practical Bengaluru guide

Questions to organize before buying a home.

Use this checklist to compare options and keep track of what you still need to confirm. Details can change, so check property-specific facts directly before relying on them.

Set your criteria

Write down the locations you are considering, your space and layout needs, travel priorities, budget range, and preferred timeline. Note which points are essential and which are flexible.

Compare the total cost

Ask what is included in the quoted price and which other costs may apply, such as registration, taxes, loan charges, maintenance, repairs, or interiors. Confirm the relevant costs for the specific property and your situation.

Clarify each listing

  • Confirm the exact locality, current availability, and asking price.
  • Ask what each stated measurement refers to; do not assume it is site area or built-up area.
  • Ask which features or amenities are included and which details came from the listing contact.
  • Note when important information was supplied or last checked, if that date is available.

Prepare for a visit

Confirm the appointment, access arrangements, and who will meet you. At the property, compare what you see with the information provided and write down questions. Take photographs only with permission.

Review documents independently

Ask which current documents apply to the specific property and have relevant questions reviewed by an independent, qualified local professional. A listing page is not proof of ownership, legal status, valuation, financial suitability, or technical condition.

Compare notes and confirm next steps

Use the same criteria for each option, keep a record of answers, and confirm unresolved details before making a commitment.

General planning information only. This guide is not legal, financial, valuation, or technical advice. Seek qualified independent advice for questions in those areas.
