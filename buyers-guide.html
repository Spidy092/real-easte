Home/Buyers guide

A practical Bengaluru guide

Buying a home is a series of questions, not one big decision.

A useful shortlist helps you compare the home, the locality, the total cost, the people involved, and the checks still needed before you commit.

Start with the full budget

Separate the home price from the other costs that may affect the decision. Consider registration, taxes, loan costs, maintenance, interiors, repairs, travel, and the cash you will need before possession or handover.

Make the shortlist useful

  • Compare location and everyday travel, not only the project name.
  • Write down the area, layout, light, ventilation, parking, and maintenance details.
  • Ask what is included in the quoted price and what is separate.
  • Keep the date and source of each important detail.

Prepare for the site visit

Walk through the home and the route to it. Ask about water, power backup, access, construction status, maintenance, nearby services, and the conditions that affect daily life. Take notes and photographs only when the property representative permits it.

Ask the document questions

For a purchase, confirm which documents and approvals apply to the specific property and who can review them. A property page can help you prepare questions, but legal and financial decisions should be reviewed with qualified professionals.

Use this as a conversation guide. It is not legal or financial advice, and the client should review this page with the right local professional before launch.

Ready to compare?

Share your preferred locality, property type, budget, and timeline. The property team can tell you what is available and which details need a closer conversation.
